# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Parents of prospective students in São Luís, Brazil — families evaluating enrollment for children aged 2–14 (Educação Infantil through Ensino Fundamental Anos Finais). They arrive with a decision to make: is this the right school for my child? They are middle-to-upper-middle-class, and value both academic rigor and a caring environment grounded in Christian values. Many visit on mobile during commute or downtime. Digital literacy varies widely.

## Product Purpose

Colégio Sagrado do Coração de Jesus is a Catholic school with over 25 years of tradition in São Luís. This site is the school's institutional face: it exists to earn trust, communicate the school's values and differentiators (qualified faculty, Catholic formation, modern infrastructure), and convert parent attention into enrollment inquiries or booked visits. Success means a parent finishes the page confident in the school's prestige, warmth, and fit for their family — and takes the enrollment action.

## Brand Personality

Prestigious, warm, traditional. The school speaks with institutional confidence and genuine care. It does not shout or hustle; it invites and reassures. The tone is similar to a respected institution that has earned its reputation over decades and knows it — but one that also sees each family as a relationship, not a transaction. Faith and values are present without being exclusionary.

## Anti-references

- Generic school template sites: clip-art-heavy, low-budget edu portals with too many tables, gradients from the 2005 era, accordion-everything layouts
- Cold corporate SaaS: dark navy backgrounds, blue gradients, startup-style stats widgets, hero-metric template layouts
- Loud edtech startups: Duolingo-style gamification, cartoon characters, neon colors, playful-chaotic type hierarchies

The school should look like what it is: a serious, respected institution that parents are proud to entrust with their children — not a tech product or a regional school trying to look trendy.

## Design Principles

1. **Institutional trust before conversion**: every design decision should first reinforce credibility and warmth; the CTA follows naturally from trust, not from urgency pressure.
2. **Prestige is in the details**: restraint in color, generous whitespace, and typographic refinement signal quality more than decoration does.
3. **Parents are the user, children are the reason**: the visual tone should feel worthy of the responsibility parents are placing in the school — mature, warm, not playful.
4. **Show, don't certify**: differentiation through imagery, testimonials, and real program content — not badge lists or "award-winning" copy.
5. **Accessible to all families**: WCAG 2.1 AA minimum; the site should be readable by grandparents, work on a 5-year-old Android phone, and function with screen readers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **Target:** WCAG 2.1 AA
- Text contrast ratios ≥ 4.5:1 for body text, ≥ 3:1 for large text
- All interactive elements keyboard-navigable with visible focus indicators
- Images with meaningful alt text; decorative images marked `aria-hidden`
- Reduced motion: animations should respect `prefers-reduced-motion`
- Language: Portuguese (pt-BR), `lang="pt-BR"` on the HTML element
- Touch targets ≥ 44×44px for mobile parents
